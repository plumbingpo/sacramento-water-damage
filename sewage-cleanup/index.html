Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. When it happens in Sacramento, Sacramento Restoration Co is available 24/7 with technicians trained for this work, proper protective gear and disinfectants registered with the EPA, everything needed for a safe decontamination.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Our systematic approach delivers quality results on every project. Here's how we handle sewage cleanup from start to finish:

1

Safety Assessment

We start by measuring how far the sewage contamination reaches, noting any safety hazards and setting up containment so clean areas are not cross-contaminated.

2

All standing sewage is extracted. Carpet and its padding, insulation, drywall and other contaminated porous items are removed, then disposed of properly as biohazard waste.

3

All remaining surfaces are thoroughly cleaned and disinfected using EPA-registered antimicrobial solutions. Airborne contaminants and pathogens are captured by HEPA air scrubbers.

4

Dry-Out and Deodorizing

We dry out any moisture that remains using commercial equipment. Industrial deodorization eliminates sewage odors. Finally, a clearance test verifies that people can safely use the area again.

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
  • Route sump pump and gutter water away from the sewer line, which can back up when storm flow overloads it
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Protect against municipal sewer backups by having a backwater valve fitted where your line connects
  • Wipes clog sewer lines even when the label says flushable, so only toilet paper goes down the toilet
  • Every 2 to 3 years, get a sewer camera run through the line to spot developing issues

Warning Signs for Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Family members falling ill, especially with gastrointestinal trouble, for no obvious reason
  • Strong sewage or rotten egg odors coming from drains or lower levels
  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix
  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path
  • More than one drain in the house overflowing or backing up together
  • Carpet or flooring contaminated when a toilet overflowed with solid waste

Why Professional Sewage Cleanup Matters

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.
