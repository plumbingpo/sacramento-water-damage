Risk Assessment

Commercial Water Damage Risk Assessment and Prevention

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions on their properties. Injuries, lawsuits, and damaged property can all trace back to a sudden failure from inadequate waterproofing, concealed moisture, or plumbing past its prime. Using thermal imaging and moisture monitoring equipment, Sacramento Restoration Co performs professional risk assessments that check how vulnerable each area is to water getting in. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

These assessments serve a dual purpose: they protect people and property by identifying vulnerabilities, and they demonstrate due diligence that can protect you legally if an incident occurs. Courts and insurance companies look favorably on property owners who maintain regular professional inspections and act on expert recommendations. Our recommendation is a moisture assessment once a year for every commercial property, and more frequent checks in high-risk spots like mechanical rooms, basements, and older plumbing systems.
