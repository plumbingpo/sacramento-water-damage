Signs It Is Time for Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Ceiling drywall that sags or bulges, or paint that bubbles after rain
  • Damaged or displaced flashing around roof penetrations, valleys or edges
  • Water in the lowest level after storm drains back up or overflow
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • Water pooling in the attic, around the chimney or near skylights
  • Shingles that are gone, cracked or curled up when you look from the yard
  • New brown rings or damp marks on walls and ceilings once the storm passes

Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear so water drains properly in heavy rain
  • Before storm season each year, get the roof inspected and its problem areas repaired
  • Good attic insulation and ventilation stop ice dams from forming along the roof edge
  • Learn where the main water shutoff is and how to close it if pipes are damaged in a storm
  • Cut back limbs that hang over or close to the house so wind cannot throw them into it
  • Take dated photos of the house every year, since insurers ask about prior condition
